## What do you need help with?

### Prescription scanning and AI

Prescription-reading results can be wrong. Check the original prescription and ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking medication or changing a dose.

### Reminders

Check Notification and Alarm permissions in device settings. If permission is off, the app cannot deliver all reminders.

### Subscriptions

Renewal, cancellation, and refunds are managed by the App Store or Google Play. You can restore a purchase from the app's subscription area.

### Account and data

You can delete your account immediately in the app, or submit a request if you cannot access the app — see the "Request account deletion" link in the page footer for full instructions.

## Not for emergencies

Thuốc ơi does not replace medical advice or emergency services. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare provider immediately.
